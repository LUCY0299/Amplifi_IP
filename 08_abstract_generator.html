本新型係關於一種無線充電裝置，主要包含一充電線圈模組及一控制模組。該充電線圈模組具有一初級線圈，用以與受電裝置之次級線圈進行電磁感應式無線電力傳輸。該控制模組電性連接至充電線圈模組，並包含一頻率調適電路及一過溫保護電路，前者依據負載狀態動態調整工作頻率，後者於溫度超過預定閾值時自動切斷電源，從而確保充電過程之安全性與效率。此外，本新型另設有一異物偵測模組，可即時偵測充電區域內之非受電物體，並立即停止充電操作，有效防止誤充造成之損害。本新型具有結構簡單、安全性高及充電效率佳等優點，適用於各種需要無線充電功能之電子裝置。

🌐 英文摘要

The present utility model relates to a wireless charging device comprising a charging coil module and a control module. The charging coil module includes a primary coil configured for electromagnetic inductive wireless power transmission to a secondary coil of a power-receiving device. The control module, electrically connected to the charging coil module, incorporates a frequency adaptation circuit and an over-temperature protection circuit. The former dynamically adjusts the operating frequency according to load conditions, and the latter automatically cuts off the power supply when the temperature exceeds a predetermined threshold, thereby ensuring charging safety and efficiency. Additionally, a foreign object detection module is provided to instantly detect non-receiving objects in the charging area and immediately terminate charging operations, preventing damage caused by accidental charging.
